Every blog starts somewhere. This is where mine starts.

## Why a blog?

Writing is how I think. Not the clean, polished kind of thinking that ends up in documentation — the messy, exploratory kind that figures out what I actually believe about something.

This blog is a space for that. Expect posts about:

- Things I'm building and why
- Ideas I'm chewing on
- Technical explorations and rabbit holes
- Anything else that seems worth putting into words

## What this site is

Built with **Hugo**, styled from scratch with a custom theme. The aesthetic is deliberately retro — pixel fonts, neon colors, the ghost of a CRT. It felt right.
